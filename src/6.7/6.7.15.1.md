# Release notes Shopware 6.7.15.1

## Abstract

Shopware 6.7.15.1 ships 12 closed issues with corrections across rule evaluation, checkout and GARAN handling, inventory limits, theme asset cleanup, and sales channel validation. It also adds an optional inline legal guarantee notice in the checkout and improves support for transactional sales channel types.

## System requirements

* tested on PHP 8.2, 8.4 and 8.5
* tested on MySQL 8, MySQL 9.7, MariaDB 11 and MariaDB 12

## Improvements

(No notable improvements in this patch release)

## Fixed bugs

**Most important bug fixes**

* Fixed line item rules so custom, credit, and extension items are evaluated correctly, preventing affected rules from incorrectly hiding shipping methods or blocking promotions ([#20673](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/issues/20673))
* Fixed order confirmation emails during blue-green updates when migrating GARAN mail labels ([#20983](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/issues/20983))
* Fixed the Administration so product variants correctly inherit and display their parent product's GARAN confirmation setting ([#20363](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/issues/20363))
* Fixed theme cleanup so cached storefront pages retain their CSS and JavaScript assets for a 24-hour grace period after a theme change ([#21010](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/issues/21010))
* Fixed digital products so their configured maximum purchase quantity is respected in the product page, cart, and backend ([#20990](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/20990))
