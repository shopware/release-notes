# Release notes Shopware 6.6.10.24

## Abstract

Shopware 6.6.10.24 is a patch release with fixes ~60 bugs. Additionally, the EU act for harmonised guarantee label and legal guarantee notice (GARAN) was added.

## System requirements

* tested on PHP 8.2 and 8.4
* tested on MySQL 8 and MariaDB 11

## Improvements

(No notable improvements in this patch release)

## Fixed bugs

* Fixed product export timeouts by switching to keyset pagination and removing the repeated per-batch count query ([#17887](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/17887))
* Fixed PDF documents and digital product downloads by keeping access rules blue-green safe and granting download access on 6.8 ([#19864](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/19864), [#18932](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/18932))
* Fixed the storefront losing cookie consent and showing stale search terms when switching between sales channels or domains ([#19568](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/19568), [#18915](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/18915))
* Fixed video playback resetting when resizing the browser or rotating a device in product and CMS galleries ([#19600](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/19600))
* Fixed promotion discounts being applied incorrectly when the cart total is below a configured fixed price ([#18960](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/pull/18960))

See all fixed bugs in this release: https://github.com/shopware/shopware/releases/tag/v6.6.10.24?closed=1
